Interviewer: Tell me about the dashboard you use.
P06: It is colourful and it is always red somewhere. New staff panic. After a month you learn to ignore the colours and look at the raw logs instead.
Interviewer: Why the raw logs?
P06: Because I can see what actually happened. The dashboard summarises, and the summary hides the one line that matters. I trust the logs, I do not really trust the scoring.
