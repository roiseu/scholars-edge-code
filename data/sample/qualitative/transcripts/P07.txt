Interviewer: How would you describe the main dashboard to a new colleague?
P07: Honestly? The dashboard is basically a slot machine. It flashes, it makes noise, and every so often it pays out a real incident. You keep pulling the lever because you might miss the one that matters.
Interviewer: Does that affect how you feel at the end of a shift?
P07: Completely drained. It is not the hard incidents that tire you, it is the hundreds of meaningless ones in between.
