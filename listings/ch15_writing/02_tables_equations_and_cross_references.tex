The F1-score is the harmonic mean of precision $P$ and recall $R$:
\begin{equation}
  F_1 = \frac{2PR}{P + R},
  \label{eq:f1}
\end{equation}
where $P = TP/(TP+FP)$ and $R = TP/(TP+FN)$.

As \cref{tab:results} shows, the gap is small; \cref{eq:f1} defines the metric
and \cref{fig:results} visualizes the trade-off.
